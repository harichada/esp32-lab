— Electronic dice: OLED + shake to roll (real firmware) —
Two dice with a tumbling animation on the OLED. Press ROLL (Enter), or "shake" the MPU6050 by quickly dragging one of its accelerometer sliders. Doubles are called out and every 10 rolls the Serial tab prints the distribution of totals.
