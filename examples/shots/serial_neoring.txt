— NeoPixel ring light show (WS2812B) —
Adafruit_NeoPixel driving a 16-LED WS2812B ring on GPIO5: rainbow wheel, fire, comet and breathing effects. Key m switches the effect; the knob sets the brightness. Every show() frame is sent to the virtual ring, so the colours are exactly what the library computed (GRB wire order).
