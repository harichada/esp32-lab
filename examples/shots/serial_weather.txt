— OLED weather station: BME280 + graphs (real firmware) —
Reads temperature, humidity and pressure from the BME280 once a second and shows an overview, temperature and pressure history graphs, and a pressure-trend forecast. Key n (PAGE) switches pages. Select the BME280 and drag its sliders to change the weather. (The DHT22 has no virtual part yet, so this uses the BME280.)
