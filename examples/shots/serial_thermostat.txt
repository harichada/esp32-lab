— DHT22 thermostat (LCD + heater/fan) —
A DHT22 (GPIO27) read with the Adafruit DHT library every 2 s; the 16×2 LCD shows temperature, humidity and the set point. UP/DOWN arrows change the set point; the red HEAT LED runs below it and the blue FAN LED above it (0.5 °C hysteresis). Select the DHT22 and drag its temperature slider to watch the thermostat react.
